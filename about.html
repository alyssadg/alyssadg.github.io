hey! i'm alyssa.


i'm a junior at Purdue University double-majoring in Network Engineering Technology and Data Analytics, Technologies & Applications, with minors in Advanced Global Technology and Organizational Leadership. i'm also working through a Data Science certificate through Purdue.

my ambition is to become a network engineer or system administrator or database administrator in the airline and aviation industry, where the system needs to be faultless all the time for those who depend on it. i am interested in the thought of working with technology and networks internationally, systems which connect the whole world together and not just an office in one organization.

i like being hands-on: running cables, configuring routers, building systems from the ground up. i want things to work, and i want them built well.
